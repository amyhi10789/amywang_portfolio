curious about my hobbies?

I dabble in quite a lot of things besides academic work.

- music / singing: I love listening to many different genres of music (more on below), and learning about music history. I also sing in the EPS choir, and with a couple friends at EPS in a small band called "The Arezzos." We regularly practice together and perform at EPS hosted events. Outside of EPS, I take vocal lessons to train my voice to the next level.

- k-pop: One of my favorite music genres is k-pop. What I love about it is that it's not just one genre, it can take on a variety of genres, and is a great way to get exposed to different subgenres of music while staying within one larger category. I have a lot of respect and admiration for k-pop idols, and enjoy the music they put out very much!

- fashion / streetwear: I'm interested in fashion / styling, and I like experimenting with different combinations/layers of clothing. Browsing online stores and thinking about what might layer well with my current items, and what could be a great addition to my wardrobe is quite fun for me! I have also slowly been improving my fashion sense over the years 😁 I've definitely had many questionable fashion moments while discovering my personal style...

- watching tv dramas: I love discovering new tv series to watch, as they are a great way to help me unwind after a long day.
